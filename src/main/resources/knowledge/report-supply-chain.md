# Supply Chain and Inventory Report

## Stockouts
**CozyHeat Space Heater**: Supplier shipment delayed by about three weeks. Two regions had no stock for most of the month. A new shipment is confirmed and stock will return gradually. Expedited freight is available at extra cost.

**TrailRunner Shoes**: Sizes in the middle of the range sold out early and replenishment is slow. Reorder quantities were set too low.

## Supplier changes
**Shield Phone Case**: Moved to a new manufacturer with better quality. Unit cost increased and the retail price was raised by about 12% to protect margin. Competing cases did not raise prices.

## Healthy stock
**Volt Power Bank** and **Aurora Wireless Earbuds** have good stock cover. Growth could be limited if demand continues to rise and reorders are not increased.

## Recommendations from operations
- Increase safety stock for seasonal products and products with a single supplier.
- Review reorder points for footwear sizes every month.
- Share inventory status with marketing so campaigns are paused for out-of-stock items.
