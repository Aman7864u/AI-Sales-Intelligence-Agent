# Product Catalog and Positioning Notes

## Electronics
**Aurora Wireless Earbuds** (price 89). Our flagship audio product and the main traffic driver for the Electronics category. Strong reviews on battery life. Main competitors are low-priced earbuds from marketplace sellers.

**Nimbus Smart Speaker** (price 129). Mid-range voice assistant speaker launched two years ago. Sound quality is good but the product has not been refreshed since launch. Competes directly with newer speakers from large brands that include a display or better microphones. Customers mostly buy it as a gift.

**Pulse Fitness Band** (price 59). Entry-level wearable. Steady seller with low return rate. Sold frequently together with the FlexFit Yoga Mat and HydroMax Bottle.

**Vertex Monitor 27in** (price 289). Premium monitor for home offices. Low volume, high revenue per order. Sales follow office and desk purchases such as the ErgoLift Standing Desk.

## Home & Kitchen
**ChefMate Air Fryer** (price 119). Best seller in Home & Kitchen with consistent demand across all regions.

**BrewPro Coffee Maker** (price 99). Stable product with repeat buyers for filters and accessories.

**PureAir Purifier** (price 179). Demand is driven by air quality news and recommendations. Low return rate.

**CozyHeat Space Heater** (price 69). Seasonal product with a single overseas supplier. Demand depends heavily on availability: when stock runs low the listing is deprioritised in search and sales fall quickly.

## Fitness
**FlexFit Yoga Mat** (price 35). Entry product, frequent add-on purchase.

**IronCore Dumbbells** (price 149). Heavy item with high shipping cost, low volume but good margin.

**TrailRunner Shoes** (price 110). Footwear with size-related returns. Sales are sensitive to size availability, because missing sizes hurt conversion.

**HydroMax Bottle** (price 25). Low price, very high volume, popular for bundles.

## Office
**ErgoLift Standing Desk** (price 449). Highest price item in the catalog. Sales rise when the remote-work content campaign is running.

**Lumina Desk Lamp** (price 45). Stable seller, often bought with desks and monitors.

**NoteMaster Notebook Set** (price 18). Low price stationery. Demand is tied to back-to-school and office restock periods and slows outside them.

**ClearView Whiteboard** (price 79). Niche product bought mostly by schools and small offices. Product is mature, has no marketing support and has been losing share to cheaper marketplace alternatives.

## Accessories
**Urban Backpack** (price 65). Reliable seller, steady across regions.

**Shield Phone Case** (price 22). High-volume commodity product, very price sensitive. Customers compare against dozens of similar cases and switch quickly when the price rises.

**Volt Power Bank** (price 39). Growing product, benefits from travel season and positive review velocity.

**Sonic Charging Cable** (price 15). Low price impulse purchase, often added at checkout.
