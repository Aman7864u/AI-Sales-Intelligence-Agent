# Monthly Business Review: Most Recent Complete Month

## Overall
Revenue for the most recent complete month was mixed. Growth in audio and power accessories was offset by declines in several mature or supply-constrained products. The sales team should focus on understanding the decliners rather than the overall total.

## Product observations
**Nimbus Smart Speaker**: A competitor launched a cheaper speaker with a display in the middle of the month. Our listing lost its "best seller" badge and comparison pages now rank the competitor first. Review score is unchanged, so the decline looks like competitive pressure and not a quality issue.

**CozyHeat Space Heater**: Out of stock in two regions for most of the month because of a delayed supplier shipment. The product was effectively not for sale there. Demand should recover once stock is back.

**ClearView Whiteboard**: Continues a multi-month decline. No marketing support, no promotions, and cheaper alternatives are winning. The team agrees the product is a candidate for clearance or discontinuation.

**Shield Phone Case**: Revenue fell after the supplier change led to a price increase. Conversion on the product page dropped soon after the new price went live.

**NoteMaster Notebook Set**: Mild decline as the back-to-school period ended. This is expected seasonal behaviour.

**TrailRunner Shoes**: Several popular sizes were unavailable for part of the month, which reduced conversion.

**Aurora Wireless Earbuds**: Strong growth after the influencer campaign and a good review streak.

**Volt Power Bank**: Growth from travel demand and restocking in all regions.

**ErgoLift Standing Desk**: Growth supported by the remote-work content campaign.

## Open questions
Leadership wants to know whether the Nimbus Smart Speaker needs a refreshed model or a price response, and whether the ClearView Whiteboard should be cleared out.
