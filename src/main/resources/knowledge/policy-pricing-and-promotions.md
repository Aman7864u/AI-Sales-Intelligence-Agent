# Pricing and Promotions Policy

## Discount limits
- Sales managers can approve discounts of up to 15% on any product.
- Discounts between 15% and 25% need approval from the Head of Sales.
- Discounts above 25% are only allowed for clearance items and need finance approval.

## Clearance rule
A product becomes a clearance candidate when its revenue has declined for three consecutive months and no stock or supply issue explains the drop. Clearance candidates can be discounted up to 40% for a limited period and are reviewed for discontinuation afterwards.

## Bundles
Bundles of two to three products can be created with a combined discount of up to 12% without extra approval. Bundles should include one fast-moving product and one slow-moving product.

## Price changes
Any price increase above 5% must be tested on a subset of traffic first. If conversion drops by more than 10% during the test, the increase must be rolled back or offset by a promotion. Commodity accessories should be price matched against the top three marketplace sellers.

## Competitive response
When a competitor launches a directly competing product, the product owner has 14 days to propose a response: a price move, a bundle, a refreshed model or a repositioning. Marketing support should be restored for products that are still strategically important.
