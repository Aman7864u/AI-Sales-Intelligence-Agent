# Marketing Campaign Report

## Active campaigns
**Influencer partnership for Aurora Wireless Earbuds.** Three creators reviewed the product. Click-through and conversion both increased. Budget is approved to continue.

**Remote-work content campaign.** Blog posts and short videos aimed at home-office buyers. Credited for growth in the ErgoLift Standing Desk and a smaller lift for the Vertex Monitor 27in and Lumina Desk Lamp.

**Email promotions.** A weekly newsletter promotes one featured product. Newsletters with a bundle offer perform better than single-product newsletters.

## Paused or unsupported products
**ClearView Whiteboard** has had no paid advertising and no email feature for several months.
**Nimbus Smart Speaker** advertising budget was cut earlier this year to fund the earbuds campaign. The speaker has had no new creative since launch.

## Learnings
- Bundles (for example Pulse Fitness Band with FlexFit Yoga Mat and HydroMax Bottle) lift average order value.
- Products hit by stockouts should not be advertised until stock is confirmed, to avoid wasted spend.
- Price-sensitive commodity products such as the Shield Phone Case respond better to price matching than to advertising.
